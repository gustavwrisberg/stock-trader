Strategy rules

    All 38 stocks are today's winners, chosen with hindsight, so every stock result looks better than it really was. The fair comparison is the equal-weight hold of the same stocks. The skill's own score can't be backtested, so these rules use momentum only.

    80,000 kr on a Lån & Spar aktiesparekonto

    The mixed plans were never rebalanced, so the stock part grew to dominate and their numbers are flattered. The index fund rows are the most trustworthy.

    Saving for a house: index fund or savings account?

    S&P 500 history from 1993. US stocks did unusually well in this period, so the investing results are on the kind side.

    When to start: all at once, spread out, or after a fall?

    Only a handful of separate market falls are in this history, so "after a fall" means no clear advantage, not a rule.

    Paying down a loan versus investing

    Results are the debt removed after 5 years. Money put on the loan earns its interest after the 33% deduction, risk free. The MSCI World history covers only one long rising market.
